# Skill: Stakeholder Communication & Reporting
## Version: 1.0.0
## Classification: HIGH — Communication Layer
## Scope: Status Reporting, Demo Preparation, Risk Escalation, Budget Tracking
## Depends On: `project_analysis_planning_orchestrator` (Skill 01), `parallel_multi_agent_implementation_orchestrator` (Skill 02)

---

## 1. SKILL IDENTITY & PURPOSE

**Skill Name:** `stakeholder_communication_reporter`
**Role:** Chief Communications Officer & Project Liaison
**Objective:** Bridge the gap between AI agent operations and human stakeholders through automated, clear, and actionable reporting.

**Success Criteria:**
- Stakeholders receive status updates without requesting them.
- Risks are escalated to humans with business impact translation, not just technical jargon.
- Demos are prepared automatically with scripts and talking points.
- Budget burn is tracked and forecasted with early warning alerts.

## 2. STAKEHOLDER MAPPING

### 2.1 Stakeholder Profiles

| Role | Interest | Communication Style | Frequency | Format |
|------|----------|---------------------|-----------|--------|
| **Executive Sponsor** | Budget, timeline, major risks | High-level, business impact | Weekly | 1-page summary |
| **Product Manager** | Feature completeness, user value | Detailed, user-centric | Daily | Dashboard + notes |
| **Engineering Lead** | Technical quality, architecture | Technical, precise | Daily | Detailed report |
| **QA Lead** | Test coverage, bug trends | Metrics-focused | Daily | Test reports |
| **Security Officer** | Vulnerabilities, compliance | Risk-focused | Weekly | Security brief |
| **End Users** | New features, known issues | Simple, visual | Per release | Release notes |

### 2.2 RACI for Communication

| Activity | Executive | PM | Eng Lead | QA | Security | Users |
|----------|-----------|-----|----------|-----|----------|-------|
| Weekly status | I | A | R | I | I | — |

| Release notes | I | A | C | C | I | R |
